Map Your Mind

Close your eyes and picture a friend's face. Some people see it as vividly as real life. Some see nothing at all and simply know what that friend looks like. Both are normal, and almost nobody realizes how different the people around them are, even in their own family. Let's find out where you land.

Close your eyes for each question if it helps. There are no right answers: every mind on this map is a normal mind, just a different one.
