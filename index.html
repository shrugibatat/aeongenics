About

Erez Aeon Batat

CONSCIOUSNESS RESEARCHER & AI CONSULTANT · FOUNDER, AEONGENICS · FORMER DIRECTOR OF INNOVATION, HP

I have spent my life on two kinds of architecture: software and the mind.

I started in tech at 19, earned a computer science degree in New York, and became Director of Innovation for HP's printing division. In 2012 a panic attack made me quit, and left me with a question I couldn't put down: what is the mechanism of the mind?

For the next decade I studied it the way an engineer studies unfamiliar software, by watching how it behaves. I interviewed hundreds of people about what happens inside their heads, and found that I have no mental imagery myself. That work became a new theory of consciousness, and my book.
